Looper Golf MCP Integration

Connect your golf data to Claude and other AI assistants. Get personalized coaching, club recommendations, course strategy, and performance analysis through natural conversation.

Connect to Claude

What You Can Do

Looper Golf gives Claude direct access to your real golf performance data — club distances, swing faults, round scores, coaching plans, and course strategy. Instead of generic advice, you get answers grounded in your actual numbers.

How far does my 7-iron carry?
Returns your average carry, total distance, ball speed, spin rate, and launch angle from your session data — in your preferred units (meters or yards).
What are my biggest swing faults and what drills should I do?
Pulls your active swing faults, traces root causes through the coaching knowledge graph, and recommends drills with progression sequences.
Draw up a strategy for me to break 80 at Drottningholm
Generates an 18-hole game plan with shot-by-shot club selections, distances, hazard warnings, and reasoning — personalized to your distances and tendencies.
How are my last few rounds?
Shows your recent round scores, courses played, and scoring trends so you can track progress.
What club should I hit from 155 meters with a headwind?
Recommends a club based on your actual distances, adjusted for wind, elevation, temperature, and hazards. Includes alternatives and warnings.
How does my driver compare to PGA Tour averages?
Compares your club stats against tour benchmarks for carry distance, ball speed, spin rate, and launch angle.

How It Works

No server-side AI. All tools return structured JSON data from your Looper account. Claude reads and interprets the data, so your answers are always grounded in your real performance numbers.

Looper Golf uses the Model Context Protocol (MCP), an open standard that lets AI assistants securely access external tools and data. When you connect Looper Golf:

  1. Claude authenticates with your Looper account via OAuth 2.1
  2. Your player profile is auto-detected — no IDs to enter
  3. Claude calls the appropriate tools based on your questions
  4. Data is returned in your preferred units (meters or yards)

Read tools return structured data from your account. Write tools (flagged clearly) let Claude take actions on your behalf — creating accounts, logging notes, sending coach messages, and setting goals. Your data stays in your Looper account and is never stored by the MCP server.

Getting Started

  1. Add the MCP server in Claude — paste https://looper-mcp-902947329020.europe-west1.run.app into Claude.ai Settings → Integrations → Add custom connector
  2. Sign in with your Looper credentials when Claude opens the login page
  3. Start asking — Claude has instant access to your shot data, coaching plans, and course strategy
No account yet? Just tell Claude: "Create a Looper Golf account for me." It will use the register_player tool to create your account on the spot — no app download needed. You can add shot data and set up your bag in the same conversation.

Supported AI Assistants

Looper Golf works with any MCP-compatible AI assistant:

  • Claude (claude.ai, iOS app, Android app, Claude Desktop, Claude Code)
  • Other MCP clients — any agent supporting the Model Context Protocol

Data & Privacy

Your golf data is stored securely in your Looper account. The MCP server acts as a read-only bridge — it queries your data on demand and never caches or stores responses. Authentication uses industry-standard OAuth 2.1 with PKCE.
